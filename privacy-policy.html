Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SmartRenoCalc ("we," "us," or "the site") collects when you use smartrenocalc.com, and how that information is handled.

1. The Calculator Collects No Personal Data

The home renovation cost calculator on this site runs entirely inside your own web browser. When you select a project type, enter a square footage, type in a ZIP code, or choose a quality tier, that information is processed locally on your device using JavaScript. None of it is transmitted to, received by, or stored on any SmartRenoCalc server. We have no database of user inputs, no record of your ZIP code, and no way to identify who calculated what. If you close your browser tab, your estimate is gone unless you printed or saved it yourself.

2. Advertising and Cookies

SmartRenoCalc displays advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. This is sometimes called interest-based or personalized advertising.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendor cookies by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page.
  • Disabling cookies in your browser settings will prevent most ad-related tracking, though it may also affect how other websites function.

3. Standard Web Server Logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as browser type, general geographic region, and pages visited, for security and performance monitoring. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

4. Children's Privacy

SmartRenoCalc is intended for general audiences planning home improvement projects and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or regulatory reasons. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@smartrenocalc.com.
