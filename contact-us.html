About the calculator

SmartRenoCalc is a free, independent cost estimator for U.S. homeowners planning a kitchen, bathroom, basement, roof, attic, or whole-house renovation. It runs entirely in your browser, so there's no account to create and nothing you enter is stored.

Each estimate starts from 2026 national cost-per-square-foot data for your project type and quality tier, then applies a regional multiplier based on the first digit of your ZIP code. The total splits roughly 45% materials and 55% labor, with a 12% contingency on top. If a number looks wrong for your area, that's exactly the kind of message we want to get.

What to include

  • If you're reporting a calculation that looks off, include the project type, ZIP code, square footage, and quality tier you entered so we can reproduce it.
  • If you're a contractor, publisher, or journalist, let us know in your subject line so we can route your message appropriately.
  • We read every message, though we can't guarantee an individual reply to every one.
